Enter current project-control measurements to surface conditions that may put contract delivery under stress. This is a transparent monitoring checklist, not a trained prediction model or a guarantee an abandonment will—or will not—happen.

Decision support only. Results are uncalibrated screening flags, not a probability, legal finding, or substitute for a quantity surveyor, engineer, commercial lead, or contract administrator. What the screen does and does not mean
  • Each flag is shown with its measurement and rule; the illustrative thresholds and points are not calibrated statistical weights.
  • Workers present are compared with the planned shift/work package. Staffing is contextual and should be interpreted alongside work type and sequence.
  • Payment age, change orders, cost forecast, and schedule progress must use the same reporting date and approved contract records.
  • Get a project-controls professional to review any high-concern output before making a procurement, termination, or funding decision.
